About Us

What is this app about?

Our app is designed to help you explore the best of Moldova by providing detailed information about must-see locations, places to rest, and a variety of activities. Whether you're looking for historical sites, outdoor adventures, or local cultural experiences, this app offers categorized recommendations like churches, camping, fishing, and more.

How do I find places to visit in Moldova?

You can use our intuitive search feature to discover places based on categories such as churches, camping, fishing, and others. You can also explore different regions and filter the results according to your interests and preferences. Simply tap on the category or use the search bar to start your journey.

Can I find places for outdoor activities like camping and fishing?

The app includes a variety of outdoor locations for camping, hiking, fishing, and other recreational activities. You can find camping sites in natural reserves, fishing spots along rivers like the Dniester, and scenic locations perfect for hiking. Each location comes with detailed information, photos, and tips.

What makes this app different from other travel apps?

Our app is specifically focused on Moldova, offering curated recommendations for unique experiences that highlight the country's natural beauty, culture, and traditions. We also provide practical features such as categorized filters (e.g., churches, camping, fishing) and personalized recommendations based on your interests.
